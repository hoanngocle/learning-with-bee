## Brand & Style

This design system is crafted specifically for early childhood education, targeting 6-year-old Vietnamese children learning English phonics alongside their parents. The aesthetic leans into a joyful, tactile "toy box" visual language—warm, cheerful, deeply encouraging, and frictionless.

Key emotional anchors:
- **Playful Tactility:** Elements feature physical, toy-like button depths, bold colorful borders, and reassuring press displacements that provide immediate visual satisfaction.
- **Cognitive Clarity:** Generous spacing, minimal textual clutter, high-contrast typography, and single-purpose interactive modules prevent cognitive overload for young learners.
- **Vibrant Progression:** A multi-chromatic level architecture (Levels 1 through 5) provides distinct, identifiable visual milestones that feel like stepping stones in an engaging learning adventure.

## Colors

The color system is organized into a primary energetic CTA palette, cognitive brand blues, soft contextual canvas surfaces, and a five-tier chromatic level spectrum.

### Surface Tiers
- **Main Canvas (`#FFFCF5`):** A soft, buttery off-white background that prevents eye fatigue.
- **Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity white backing for vocabulary blocks, interactive cards, and headers.
- **Section Enclosures:** Warm Cream (`#FFF4D8`) for illustration stages and games; Soft Sky (`#E9F6FF`) for guided learning modules.

### Interactive & Brand Actions
- **Primary CTA (`#FF8A35`):** High-energy orange for core calls-to-action (e.g., "Start Learning"), anchored by a physical `#E56D27` depth bevel.
- **Learning & Audio Blue (`#2769BB` / `#244F97`):** Directs ear and voice interactions, pronunciation triggers, and primary headings.

### Level Theming Spectrum
Each learning level carries a distinct hue pair (`accent` border/tag and `light` background tint):
- **Level 1 (The Alphabet):** Accent `#22A9C2` / Light `#E4FAFF`
- **Level 2 (Short Vowels):** Accent `#F19B33` / Light `#FFF4E2`
- **Level 3 (Long Vowels):** Accent `#6AAD63` / Light `#EBFAE9`
- **Level 4 (Consonant Blends):** Accent `#9871C3` / Light `#F4EAFF`
- **Level 5 (Letter Combinations):** Accent `#E46B78` / Light `#FFF0F0`

## Typography

The typography leverages heavy rounded forms to maintain warmth, friendliness, and legibility for early readers and bilingual Vietnamese parents.

- **Weight Emphasis:** Titles and interactive actions consistently use `800` and `900` font weights to reinforce character recognition and visual confidence.
- **Bilingual Clarity:** Body descriptions pairing English and Vietnamese translations pair standard weights (`400`–`600`) with generous `1.5`–`1.65` line heights for immediate comprehension.
- **Word Highlights:** Target vocabulary words display high-contrast scaling with the first letter optionally tinted in vibrant orange (`#FF8A35`) to emphasize initial phoneme awareness.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows an 8px modular base rhythm inside a centered container with a maximum width of `1280px`.

- **Desktop (≥ 1200px):** 12-column fluid grid. Section padding is set to `3rem` to `4.5rem`. The level selector organizes into 5 uniform columns. The vocabulary flashcard utilizes an asymmetric 2-column split (45% art staging, 55% phonetic prompt and action buttons).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** Adapts the level selector to a wrap-grid (3 then 2 cards) and adjusts hero margins to `2.5rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Reflows all interactive modules into single-column vertical stacks. Margin defaults to `1rem` on phone viewports with touch targets enforced at a minimum height of `48px`.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system favors physical, tactile 3D extrusion over diffuse atmospheric blur:

- **3D Toy Button Extrusions:** Primary interactive buttons apply a solid `0 6px 0` bottom shelf (`#E56D27`). On active state (`:active`), the element translates downwards by `4px`, and the shadow reduces to `0 2px 0`, simulating a mechanical toy switch.
- **Asymmetric Base Borders:** Unit cards and selection blocks incorporate a structural bottom border of `5px` to `6px` tinted to the level's accent color, providing grounded physical depth.
- **Floating Toy Badges:** Hero cards and mascot bubbles incorporate subtle solid drop ledges (`0 10px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`) paired with low-opacity ambient blurs (`0 15px 30px rgba(36, 59, 90, 0.08)`) and slight rotational offsets (`-7deg` to `8deg`) to create a playful sticker-like look.

## Shapes

Shapes utilize a soft, chunky curvature that feels safe, organic, and friendly:

- **Full Capsules (`999px`):** Eyebrow indicators, category labels, progress chips, and header translation pills.
- **Buttons (`16px` – `20px`):** Chunky rounded rectangles engineered to support 3D bottom bevels without corner clipping.
- **Flashcards & Feature Wrappers (`24px` – `28px`):** Main interactive lesson panels and quiz containers use expansive border radii to emphasize containment and focus.
- **Unit Tiles (`20px` – `22px`):** Rounded modules framed by `3px` side/top borders and `6px` bottom anchor borders.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA (`.btn-primary`):** Bold orange fill (`#FF8A35`), white text, `20px` radius, `0 6px 0 #E56D27` depth shadow. Paddings `16px 28px`. Translates `4px` down when pressed.
- **Audio Listen Action (`.btn-listen`):** Energetic blue fill (`#2769BB`), white text with native audio icon prefix (`🔊`), `14px` radius, `0 4px 0 #1D4F8E` bevel.
- **Secondary / Navigation Controls (`.btn-secondary`):** White background, `2px solid #D6E8F6`, ink text (`#4B6F93`), rounded `12px` to `16px`.

### Badges & Category Chips
- **Eyebrow Badges:** High-contrast pill containers (`999px` radius) with soft pastel fills (e.g. `#FFF2C6` with `#DF9540` text for games; `#E9F6FF` with `#3987B8` text for lessons).
- **Progress Counter:** Distinct capsule chip (`#FFFFFF` background, `#243B5A` bold count, subtle outline) indicating current module index (`1 / 5`).

### Level Selection Cards
- **Card Shell:** Background `#FFFFFF`, rounded `21px`, `3px` solid stroke with `6px` bottom anchor border using the assigned level accent color.
- **Active State:** Surface shifts to matching level light background tint (`var(--light)`), with a `2px` scale accentuation.
- **Icon / Mascot Framing:** Top-centered oversized emoji or vector character (`55px` frame) paired with uppercase unit numbering.

### Flashcard Module
- **Container:** Rounded `28px` wrapper in Soft Sky (`#E9F6FF`), interior split card in clean `#FFFFFF`.
- **Art Staging Area:** Soft Cream `#FFF4D8` backing, rounded `20px`, holding large vocabulary illustrations (`100px`+ scale).
- **Phonics Typography Display:** Prominent title in `#244F97` with the lead phonetic character highlighted in `#FF8A35`, accompanied by Vietnamese subtitle translations.

### 3-Option Mini-Quiz Cards
- **Answer Selection Tile:** Uniform 3-column layout. Thick border (`3px solid #E7EDF0`), `#FFFFFF` surface, rounded `19px`, large central icon/graphic (`56px`), with legible word underneath.
- **Correct State:** Instant border transition to `#219C73` with `#EBFAE9` fill.
- **Incorrect State:** Border transition to `#E46B78` with `#FFF0EE` fill.
